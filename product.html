Questions, Answered

How do I enquire about a piece? +
Tap "Enquire About This Piece" and you'll be connected to a specialist on WhatsApp. They confirm availability, share pricing, and guide the rest personally.
Why are prices not listed? +
Availability and pricing move quickly at this end of the market. Quotes are confirmed live, one-to-one, so what you're offered is accurate the moment you ask.
How long does delivery take? +
Most pieces ship within 24–48 hours of confirmation and arrive in 3–5 business days, fully insured and discreetly packaged.
Do you deliver internationally? +
We primarily serve the United States. For other destinations, message your specialist and we'll confirm what's possible for your location.
What if the piece isn't as shown? +
You approve photographs and video of your exact piece before it ships. If what arrives doesn't match what you approved, we make it right.
Looking for something else?
Send a photograph on WhatsApp — we will find it.
